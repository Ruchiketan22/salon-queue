# Salon Queue — Brief

## Problem
On weekends, customers wait 1–2 hours inside the salon with no idea when their turn is. Some give up and leave, others argue about who arrived first, and the receptionist keeps a messy paper list. The salon needs one fair, visible queue that customers can follow from their phone.

## Users
- **Customer**: joins and tracks the queue from a phone (or is added by reception as a walk-in)
- **Receptionist**: manages the queue on a tablet
- **Barber**: sees their current customer on a phone and taps Start / Done
- **Owner**: configures services, barbers, and opening of the online queue

## Must-have features
1. Join the queue from a phone: name, phone number, 1–3 services, a barber or "Any barber"
2. Live status page: token, position, estimated wait, "Start coming" alert and "It's your turn" alert
3. Receptionist dashboard: add walk-ins, call next for a barber, mark no-show, cancel
4. Barber screen: current customer, Start / Done, On break / Available
5. Waiting-area TV: who is with which barber, plus the next 5 tokens
6. Owner settings: services (time, price, on/off), barbers on duty, open/close online queue, today's stats
7. English and Gujarati labels with a language toggle
8. Everything stays in sync across phone, tablet and TV windows within 5 seconds

## Don't build
Online payment, future-date appointments, SMS/WhatsApp notifications, loyalty points, multiple branches, customer accounts or login (staff screens use a simple PIN only), reviews.

## Look & feel
Premium salon feel: dark charcoal with gold accent, rounded cards, large readable text, mobile-first.

## Open hours (assumption used everywhere)
Salon open 10 AM – 9 PM. Online joining stops at 8:30 PM.
